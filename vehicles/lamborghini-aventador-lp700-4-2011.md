# Lamborghini Aventador LP 700-4, L539, 2011 launch specification

Built: November 2026, take-apart level 1 (with full cockpit and level-1 floorpan), 7 revision rounds.

## References that mattered

- Design patent USD672284S1 gives five orthographic elevations (2.3–6.4 mm RMS), but its tyres are explicitly
  illustrative dashed boxes (~36 mm off production track in rear view). Use factory tracks/tyre sizes and body
  landmarks instead.
- Geneva launch photos (Autoviva, Norbert Aepli) set the launch trim, Arancio Argos hue, lit Y-signature lamps
  and optional transparent three-pane engine bonnet. Petersen museum high-res photos supply unlit lamp optics,
  side intake depth, wheel casting, exhaust heat shield and centre console switchgear (note its market-specific
  amber/red side markers).

## Hard parts

- **Transparent engine bonnet.** Opaque patent slats look plausible from directly above while hiding a 50 mm
  aft height error and missing the deep X-brace and V12 bay. Verify three overlapping glass panes and parallax
  from both low rear and elevated oblique views.
- **Fixed rear quarterlight and roof-pillar intake.** Separate the small fixed side pane from the tapered black
  engine intake behind it (~370 mm long); rebuild the painted sail over it as one fair surface, not strips.
- **Rear-quarter shoulder and side intake.** Decouple the shoulder sweep from the recessed intake control point
  (sharing one caused a 6.7 mm dip over 80 mm) and check both the upper and lower legs of the auxiliary inlet
  ahead of the rear tyre.
- **Cockpit steering wheel, seats and console.** Measure steering rim thickness from two angles (~41 × 33 mm
  thumb grip), separate the orange head pad from the backrest by a dark recessed neck inside continuous black
  bolsters, and verify passenger/driver sightlines to the lower P/M/R transmission keys when raising the armrest.

## Counts and facts worth knowing first

- Rear lamps: three red Y guides per side plus a narrow unlit centre stop strip under the stowed spoiler.
- Front headlamp indicators: seven LEDs arranged four beside the white Y and three inboard (not 3 + 4).
- Diffuser: seven vanes with rear tyre tread visible through the outer bays; four closely spaced inner exhaust
  outlets inside one polygonal finisher.
- Wheels: staggered 19-inch front (255/35 ZR19) / 20-inch rear (335/30 ZR20), five paired spokes with crisp
  flat inner window borders and five lugs; open mirror span is 2,265 mm.
- Small details easily missed: right-side polygonal fuel flap, two trapezoidal outboard cowl slots ahead of
  the windscreen, four slim flank marker lenses, seven aircraft-style console toggles (hazard fourth).

## Left open

Tunnel-rail end shaping, rotary controller crown/starter guard details, two outboard windscreen-corner cowl
outlets, reverse-key (`R`) legend occlusion, flank markers and finer gathering at the inner steering seam.

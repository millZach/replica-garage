# Porsche 911 GT3 RS, 992, 2022 on

Built: November 2026, with a separate take-apart workshop copy, 74 agent hours including fixes.

## References that mattered

- The maker's six studio press photos solved to within 4 to 10 mm at the known points.
- The front wheels are turned about 12 degrees in those photos.
- The straight rear photo is not symmetric at its outer edges. The inner landmarks agree; fit to the side the
  three-quarter photos confirm.
- A drawing of the earlier 991 generation has the same name on it and the wrong shape. Keep it out.

## Hard parts

- **Rear haunch and tail.** The most rounds of anything. Patching kept moving the kink to the patch's edge.
  Rebuilding the whole rear quarter as one surface between measured curves fixed it.
- **Headlamps.** Flat at first. They need the clear cover, a recessed projector and four separate reflectors
  at depth, each placed from two views.
- **Front fender vents.** Read wrongly three times from a grazing view. The close-up shows the count and how
  the blades stack; the black base is one moulding and the blades run on into it.
- **Front pillar where roof, door and fender meet.** A repair laid over the old skin looked right from the
  front and showed its outline from the side. One skin, joined edge to edge.
- **Rear lamp bar.** It wraps round the corner. From straight behind it reads as if it stops short.

## Counts and facts worth knowing first

- Bonnet: two outlets. Front fender: two louvres each side, not three.
- Wheels: seven pairs of spokes.
- Wing end plates toe in about one degree and lean outward.
- The door's upper half is narrower than it looks; too wide and the rear haunch cannot stand out.

## Left open

A faint shading step on the painted front pillar; small differences in lamp reflectors and rear lettering.
